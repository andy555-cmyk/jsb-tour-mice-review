BUSAN ROOTS · SINCE 2018

Rooted in Busan,connected to people and places

JSB TOUR & MICE was founded in Busan in 2018 to plan travel and events

We plan conferences, exhibitions and corporate events alongside tailored journeys for international visitors, companies and groups. Our programs can include post-event travel, cultural experiences and time to unwind

Our company profile documents a Singapore roadshow, post-conference travel for international delegates and familiarization trips for overseas influencers
