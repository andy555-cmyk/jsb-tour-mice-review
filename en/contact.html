GOOD TO KNOW

Before you send, a few things to know

What if my dates or group size aren’t decided yet?

You can leave the optional fields blank. In your message, just give us a rough idea of the timing and the kind of trip or event you have in mind

Can I book on this page?

No. Sending an inquiry is a request for a consultation; no booking or payment is made. Dates, prices and availability are confirmed with JSB in a separate consultation

Is my inquiry saved automatically?

Your details are not saved before sending. Copy or download the note if you need it. After sending, the delivery service may retain the submission for a limited period
