PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy

JSB TOUR & MICE handles personal information received through its website inquiry form as set out below

Effective 29 September 2026

1. Purpose

We process personal information to review and reply to travel and event inquiries. We do not use it for any other purpose, and we will ask for your consent before any change of purpose

2. Information we collect

  • Required: name, email address and message
  • Optional: company or group, preferred date, expected group size and service of interest

Please do not include sensitive details such as passport numbers or health information in your message

3. Retention and deletion

  • We keep inquiries for one year after the consultation ends and then delete them without delay
  • If an inquiry leads to a contract, contract records are kept for the periods required by law
  • Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered, and printouts are shredded

4. Service providers and overseas transfer

We use the following providers to deliver and store inquiries

FormSubmit (formsubmit.co)
Delivers website inquiries to JSB's email
Mailplug
Hosts JSB's business email

Sending your inquiry through FormSubmit is an overseas transfer, detailed below

Recipient
FormSubmit · support@formsubmit.co
Country
Outside Korea (where the provider's servers are located; Canada, based on emails received in September 2026)
When and how
Sent over an encrypted internet connection when you press the send button
Information transferred
Everything entered in the inquiry form
Purpose and retention
Delivering your inquiry to JSB's email; kept by the service for up to 30 days
How to decline
If you do not agree on the inquiry form, nothing is sent. You can still contact us directly by phone or email

5. Sharing with third parties

We do not share personal information with third parties, except where the law requires it or investigators request it through legal procedures

6. Your rights

You may ask at any time to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information. Contact us by email or phone and we will act without delay. A legal representative or an authorised person may also make the request

7. Security measures

  • The website and inquiry transfer are protected with encryption (HTTPS)
  • Access to inquiry emails is limited to the staff who handle them
  • We do not ask for information we do not need

8. Cookies and automatic collection

This website does not use cookies or visitor analytics. Only your motion pause setting is stored temporarily in the browser and disappears when you close the window. The servers that host the website may keep access logs, such as IP addresses, for security and operation
