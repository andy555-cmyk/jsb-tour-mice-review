A JOURNEY TO REMEMBER

Ulsan · BusanA 5-day, 4-night itinerary

A 5-day, 4-night itinerary that starts at Daeunsan Healing Forest and Daewangam Park, then continues to Huinnyeoul Culture Village, Cheongsapo and Songjeong in Busan

Day by day

Sample itinerary · 5 days, 4 nights
  1. DAY 01Ulsan’s forests and sea+

    Gimhae Airport → Ulsan → National Daeunsan Healing Forest → Daewangam Park & Suspension Bridge → Ulsan Bridge Observatory

  2. DAY 02The story of whales and pottery+

    Seuldo Island → Jangsaengpo Whale Culture Village → Oegosan Onggi Village & Onggi Museum

  3. DAY 03Busan’s culture and coast+

    Huinnyeoul Culture Village → Busan Modern and Contemporary History Museum → Beach Train → Cheongsapo Daritdol Observatory → Club D Oasis

  4. DAY 04A day in Busan+

    Haedong Yonggungsa Temple → Songjeong Beach & ocean-view cafe → Millac The Market & Podium Dive → Haeundae Market

  5. DAY 05Until we meet again+

    Hotel departure → Gimhae Airport

Before you travel

  • This is a sample itinerary for tailored planning, not a bookable tour with a confirmed departure
  • Prices, accommodation, transport and what is or isn’t included are confirmed separately for your dates and group size
  • The order of visits and activities may change depending on local conditions and availability
