A 6-day itinerary through Andong (Hahoe Village, Dosan Seowon), Goryeong (Jisan-dong Ancient Tombs) and Gyeongju (Bulguksa Temple, Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond)

Day by day

Sample itinerary · 6 days
  1. DAY 01Scenery of Andong+

    Hahoe Village → Dosan Seowon → Woryeonggyo Bridge

  2. DAY 02A quiet temple+

    Bongjeongsa Temple

  3. DAY 03The age of Daegaya+

    Gayatgo Village → Jisan-dong Ancient Tombs → Daegaya Museum

  4. DAY 04Gyeongju’s ancient beauty+

    Bulguksa Temple → Seokguram Grotto → Hwangnidan-gil

  5. DAY 05Gyeongju from day to night+

    Cheonmachong Tomb → Gyeongju National Museum → Donggung Palace and Wolji Pond → Gyochon Village → Woljeonggyo Bridge
